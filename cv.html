Discourse Integrity Map live project ↗
2026
Computational research project · CIP fellowship application

Project Summary: Can we identify common ground and characterise disagreement beneath rhetoric by mapping debate spaces and aggregating claims into canonical positions? The live project explores this through an experimental "deliberative debating" interface, with accompanying visualisations, report, and corpus.

Pipeline & Implementation: Designed and implemented a five-stage computational pipeline for mapping argument structure and rhetorical patterns in public debate: transcript ingestion, claim extraction, discourse skeletonisation, rhetorical technique classification, and canonical position aggregation. Analysed 17 debates and speeches, extracting 390 rhetorical techniques against an original 191-category taxonomy and classifying 456 claims from the corpus.

Methodology & Findings: Theoretically grounded in the Aristotelian distinction between dialectic and eristic argument, the project aims to foreground how arguments are made, and the underlying structure of debate, rather than whether conclusions are correct. The system identified 37 canonical positions across wealth tax discourse, including 5 points of cross-partisan agreement. Longer term, the project aims to explore whether identified points of contention can function as deliberative "springboards" toward more constructive public reasoning.

TruthSpace: NLP Propaganda Detection & Media Bias Platform dissertation ↗
2025
MSc Dissertation · UWE Bristol · Distinction · Examiner feedback: "highly innovative"

Project Summary: Can machine learning detect rhetorical manipulation techniques in news coverage of immigration, and can those detections be combined with bias and sentiment data into an interface that helps readers think critically rather than telling them what to think?

Architecture: End-to-end NLP pipeline using python scripts to collect + process 854 UK/US news articles — revived SemEval-2020 propaganda span detection in a containerised 2025 environment, enriched with VADER, TextBlob, spaCy NER, and bias ratings from Ad Fontes, MBFC, AllSides, and Ground News.

Front-end Design: Chart.js interface surfacing rhetorical manipulation patterns and sentiment.
